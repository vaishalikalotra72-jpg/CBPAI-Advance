Before you join

How the programme runs

Joining links and the class code are in the Welcome and Onboarding email sent to your registered address.

Three platforms

  • ZoomAll live sessions, discussions and speaker interactions. One link for all five days.
  • Google ClassroomAnnouncements, learning resources and assignments. Join with your registered email ID.
  • SpandanQuizzes, polls and questions during sessions. Log in to Learn, not Teach.

Session guidelines

  1. Keep your camera on, with your face clearly visible in good light.
  2. Use your full name as your Zoom display name.
  3. Take part in every poll and quiz. They count towards attendance.
  4. Test your audio and internet connection before you join.
Certificate of completion Attend at least 75% of the sessions, take part actively on Spandan, and submit the final project.
